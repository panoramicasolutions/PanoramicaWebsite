A brief in your inbox, before every call

The problem

Reps walk into calls underprepared. The context they need sits across the calendar, the CRM and past calls, and pulling it together before each meeting takes time.

How it works

  1. It watches your calendar

    Each external meeting on the calendar triggers a brief.

  2. It gathers the context

    It pulls from your CRM, your call history and meeting transcripts, and checks each fact against the record.

  3. It emails the rep

    A morning overview at 08:00, then a detailed brief 30 minutes before each meeting.

What is included

  • Setup and your first live brief, for a single team
  • Two emails per rep on working days: one overview, then one brief per meeting
  • Calendar, CRM, call history and meeting transcripts as sources
  • Record checks before a rep sees anything

You own it once it is live.

Safeguards

Questions

Who is it for?

Reps taking five or more calls a week with real prospects, on Google Workspace (Calendar and Gmail), with a CRM and call history to draw from.

Who is it not for yet?

Teams not on Google Calendar or Gmail, teams just starting out with no real pipeline in motion, and teams with no CRM or call history yet.

When does the brief arrive?

Reps get an overview at 08:00 on working days and a detailed brief 30 minutes before each meeting.

Which tools does it work with?

Google Calendar and Gmail are required. HubSpot and Aircall are proven. Other CRMs are available on request.

What does it cost to run?

The build fee covers setup and the first live brief. LLM costs are separate and confirmed before launch.
