Questions

Before you get in touch

What is Panoramica Solutions?

Panoramica Solutions builds AI-enabled revenue systems for teams in financial services, technology and learning, inside the tools they already use, and supports them after launch. Every engagement is led by founder Lorenzo Liviero.

Who does Panoramica work with?

Revenue teams in financial services, technology and learning, from growing companies to established firms with years of CRM history.

Which CRMs do you work with?

HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio and others. We build inside the CRM you already use.

Is it a one-off build or ongoing?

Either. Every build has a defined scope and a price agreed up front. After handover, ongoing support keeps it running and improving, if you want it.

Do you publish client names or case studies?

No. Every engagement is different, and client confidentiality is part of the work. The proof on this site is what the builds themselves do, not client names.

How much do the services cost?

Pricing is shared on request. Goldmine, The Brief and Marketing Studio have fixed prices; email outreach infrastructure, the data foundation, custom builds and ongoing support are scoped individually. Revenue Architect, the self-serve diagnostic, is £149 and live soon. Inquire about pricing on any service page.
