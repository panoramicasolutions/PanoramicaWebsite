Email outreach infrastructure built around a real reason to write

For LP/capital outreach, founder-led outreach and targeted lead generation.

We build the research, personalization and message logic, plus the Gmail-on-Google-Workspace workflow and CRM handoff.

Custom scope Scope my outreach infrastructure

Who it is for

Teams with a defined market and an important list, but weak research depth, generic copy or inconsistent follow-up.

It suits founders, fundraising teams and small revenue teams that need relevant outbound conversations without making research, drafting and follow-up another full-time job.

What gets built

  • Audience, persona and signal definition
  • Account and contact research
  • Personalization logic and evidence rules
  • Message or sequence logic
  • Gmail and Google Workspace setup, with agreed sending controls
  • Review, exception and approval rules
  • CRM handoff
  • Documentation

What you get: a reviewable outreach system that turns account and contact research into message drafts or sequences and makes the CRM handoff clear.

What does not happen

  • No invented facts, and no scraped trivia presented as relevance
  • No autonomous sending without agreed controls
  • No guaranteed replies, meetings, capital or revenue
  • No hidden sending infrastructure

Use cases

LP and capital outreach

For fundraising teams writing to LPs and capital partners.

Founder-led outreach

For founders who write the first message themselves and want it to hold up.

Targeted lead generation

For teams reaching a defined list of target accounts.

How scope is set

These inputs change the work, so we agree them before quoting:

  • Audience size
  • Research depth and data availability
  • Number of personas
  • Sequence complexity
  • Sending setup
  • CRM handoff

List procurement, domain or mailbox acquisition, deliverability remediation and ongoing campaign operation are included only if explicitly scoped.
