Under the hood

Built to be explainable, checked before it's trusted.

Decay, not decree
A "hot" lead cools on a 14-day half-life, a "cold" one can warm back up on a 30-day one. Recency is modelled, not just flagged true or false.
Three-channel ghosting
Marketing, call, and email silence are scored separately, not lumped into one generic "unresponsive" flag.
Floor and ceiling
A lead in active pursuit can't silently sink below a set score floor. A lead carrying a genuine negative signal can't silently outscore a hard cap either. No invisible overrides in either direction.
Checked, not trusted blind
Outputs are scanned for claims the underlying data doesn't support before a rep ever sees them, built in after we caught our own earlier model misreading a lost deal as a live investment, and fixed the class of bug, not just the one instance.
Compliance-aware fields
For regulated clients, fields like accreditation status and source of funds pass through as structured data, not free text buried in a notes box.
