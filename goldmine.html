Questions

Common questions

Which CRM does it work with?

Pricing applies to HubSpot. Other CRMs are scoped separately.

What do the three depths change?

Core is scoring and write-back on a single pipeline, at £6,000. Full Build adds AI synthesis behind record checks and multiple pipelines, at £8,500. Extended adds custom signals and a reporting dashboard, at £11,000.

How do I know why a lead ranks where it does?

Every rank shows the signals behind it, and the scoring rules are written so your team can read and check them.

How many records do I need?

Roughly 10,000 or more contact and deal records, in a CRM your team uses daily. A new or nearly empty CRM is not a fit yet.
